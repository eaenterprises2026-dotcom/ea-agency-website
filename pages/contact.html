FAQ

Common Questions

How long does a typical project take?▼
Most automation and AI agent projects go from kickoff to live in 2–4 weeks. Website builds typically take 4–6 weeks depending on complexity. We provide detailed timelines in your proposal before we start.
Do I need technical knowledge to work with you?▼
Not at all. We handle all the technical implementation and explain everything in plain business language. You tell us your goals — we figure out how to get there. We also train your team so you can manage the systems independently after launch.
What tools and platforms do you work with?▼
We work with n8n, Make (Integromat), Zapier, OpenAI, Anthropic Claude, Google APIs, Airtable, Notion, HubSpot, Salesforce, Slack, and dozens more. If you already use a tool, we can almost certainly integrate with it.
What does a discovery call involve?▼
It's a 30-minute conversation where we learn about your business, your current workflows, and your goals. No pitch, no pressure — just a genuine conversation to see if we're the right fit. You'll leave with at least 2–3 actionable AI ideas regardless of whether you hire us.
Do you offer payment plans?▼
Yes. For projects over $2,000 we typically split payment: 50% upfront to begin, 50% on delivery. For larger projects we can arrange milestone-based payments. We want to make working together accessible — just ask.
What happens after the project is delivered?▼
All projects include 30 days of free support after delivery. We document everything and train your team. After that, you can continue on one of our monthly retainer plans for ongoing optimization, new features, and priority support.
