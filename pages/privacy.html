Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 6, 2026

Who We Are

EA Enterprises ("we", "us", "our") is an AI agency that builds websites, AI systems, and automation workflows for businesses. Our website is ea-agency-website.vercel.app. You can reach us at eaenterprises2026@gmail.com.

What Information We Collect

We collect information you voluntarily provide when you:

  • Submit our contact form (name, email, company, service interest, budget, message)
  • Chat with our AI assistant (your messages and any contact information you share)
  • Submit a testimonial or review (name, role, review text)

How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your inquiry and provide the services you requested
  • To send you a confirmation email acknowledging your submission
  • To evaluate your project needs and prepare a proposal
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties for marketing purposes

How We Store Your Information

Your information is stored securely in our database (Supabase) hosted in the United States. We use industry-standard security practices including encrypted connections and access controls. Only authorized personnel can access your data.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Correction — ask us to correct inaccurate information
  • Deletion — request that we delete your personal data
  • Objection — object to how we process your data

To exercise any of these rights, email us at eaenterprises2026@gmail.com with the subject line "Privacy Request". We will respond within 30 days.

Cookies

Our website uses browser session storage to maintain your chat conversation within a single session. This data is cleared when you close your browser tab. We do not use tracking cookies or advertising cookies.

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services to operate our website:

  • Vercel — website hosting
  • Render — backend API hosting
  • Supabase — database storage
  • Resend — email delivery
  • Anthropic — AI chat responses

Each service has its own privacy policy and data handling practices.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision. Continued use of our website after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact

For any privacy-related questions, contact us at eaenterprises2026@gmail.com.
